# Property facts - Hotel Aurora (fixture data)

Sample data for `make demo` and the tests: a shorter copy of the same
invented property described in `knowledge/property.example.md`. A real clone
of this repo fills in `knowledge/property.md` with its own facts, which then
takes over from this file - see docs/how-it-works.md.

- Name: Hotel Aurora
- Address: 1 Example Street, 1000-001 Lisbon, Portugal
- 42 rooms, reception staffed 07:00 to 23:00

## Arrival and departure

- Check-in from 15:00. Check-out by 11:00.
- Late check-out to 14:00: EUR 30 when the room is free.

## Spa

- The Aurora Spa: two treatment rooms, open 09:00 to 19:00.
- Children: the spa is for guests aged 16 and over, no exceptions, including
  the sauna.

## Food and drink

- Breakfast 07:30 to 10:30, EUR 18 per person, children under 12 free.

## Policies

- Free cancellation up to 48 hours before arrival. After that, one night.
- Pets: small dogs welcome, EUR 15 a night, please tell us in advance.

## What we cannot do

- We cannot guarantee a specific room number, only a room type.
- We do not have a pool or a gym.
