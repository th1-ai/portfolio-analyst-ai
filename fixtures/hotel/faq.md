# Guest FAQ - Hotel Aurora (fixture data)

Sample data for `make demo` and the tests. A shorter copy of
`knowledge/faq.example.md`.

## What time can I check in?

From 15:00. If you arrive earlier we will hold your bags at reception.

## Can children use the spa?

The Aurora Spa is for guests aged 16 and over, including the sauna.

## Is breakfast included?

Depends on the rate you booked. If not, EUR 18 per person, served 07:30 to
10:30.

## Can I cancel my booking?

Free up to 48 hours before arrival. Inside 48 hours we charge the first
night.
